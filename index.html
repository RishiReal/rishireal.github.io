Research

LLM-Based Inverse Recovery of Parameters from Partial Differential Equations
Computational Mechanics & Scientific AI Lab — 2026, ongoing

Designed an MCP client-server system where an LLM agent iteratively recovers thermal diffusivity α from temperature profiles governed by the 1D heat equation. The agent queries a finite difference PDE solver as a tool, applying gradient-based parameter updates with adaptive learning rate and CFL-stable time-stepping — framing inverse estimation as an agentic loop rather than a supervised task.
